  • In review · has an open pull request.
  • Ready · done and checked in your working tree, not delivered yet.
  • In progress · being worked on right now.
  • Next · the one task /flow-next would take.
  • Pending · not started yet.
  • Blocked · stopped; the reason is written next to it.
  • Completed · merged into main.
  • Groups show only when they have tasks, in this order: In review, Ready, In progress, Next, Pending, Blocked, Completed. “In flight” is gone: its three states are groups of their own.
  • Next is one row with a 2 px blue rule on the left and a bold title; no border, no fill, and no word “Next” on the row because the group header already says it.
  • Pending lists the takeable tasks first; a task that waits for another one comes after them, dimmed, with no icon and “waits for RP136” in grey. It stays a flat row that names its blocker; it is never nested under the other task.
  • Completed is folded (chevron at the right) and shows the last completed task under its header.
  • At 220 px several rows take two or three lines (title, then data below); the full text is also the tooltip. No search box.
