  • Rows look like cards: each task has a border and a fill like a button, and the next one has a blue frame, so the whole list reads as clickable. They are not: only the title tooltip answers.
  • A filter box sits at the top; it is the first thing in the tab and it is not used.
  • The segmented bar and its legend say the counts, but there is no sentence ("12 of 24 done") and no icon per row: the state is a chip on the right, which gets clipped in a narrow column.
  • Pending, Blocked and Completed are folded behind a count only: nothing says what waits for what, why something is blocked, or what finished last.
  • No freshness line: the list can be stale and nothing says so. The tab is also last in the strip today (Changes, Files, Status, Tasks).
